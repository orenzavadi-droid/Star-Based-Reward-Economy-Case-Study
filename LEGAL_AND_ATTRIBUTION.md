# Can this independent project refer to Solitaire Grand Harvest?

**Short answer:** A self-authored, clearly independent analysis of **publicly documented game mechanics** is a substantially safer approach than copying game assets, reproducing game code, claiming studio affiliation, or implying access to confidential data. However, **we have NOT obtained publisher permission** and cannot guarantee the legality or Terms-of-Service compliance of any public use. This is **not legal advice**.

## What publisher terms say

Playtika's Terms of Service (last updated **June 2026**) reserve intellectual-property rights in game content and limit the service license to **personal, non-commercial entertainment use**. The terms also restrict **using the services or data from them to develop/validate AI systems, or for other purposes not expressly allowed**, and accessing the Services to design/develop similar or competitive offerings. These clauses create **additional uncertainty** around AI-assisted analysis or publication of a game-derived model; their exact enforceability and applicability to independent commentary cannot be decided here. See https://www.playtika.com/terms-service/ (reviewed 18 November 2026).

Those contractual restrictions are **not the same thing as** a definitive court assessment of the right to make factual commentary, nominative reference or lawful critical analysis. Jurisdiction, context and use matter. We cannot certify permission just from public availability.

## Safer practices used in this package

1. Project authorship independent; no official logos, original screenshots, sounds, character art, card layouts, interface captures, or Playtika proprietary code included.
2. Game name used solely in a descriptive phrase to identify the subject of study. Public repo title can be neutral: `Star-Based Reward Tier Economy Case`.
3. No monetization, commercial sale, ad placement or implied endorsement of this project.
4. Every factual claim links to a public source with date/temporal caveat. Historical values are explicitly dated.
5. Every new numerical star threshold, segment, prize EV, participation and result is marked **hypothetical**.
6. No internal data, no bypassing access controls, no decompilation and no exposed personal identifiers.

## Publishing recommendation

- **Private feedback / Zoom:** show the independent one-pager and Excel; discuss hypothetical models. Avoid showing third-party screenshots or copying original game images.
- **Public GitHub:** the proposed neutral repository name, original self-authored diagrams, short attributed factual references and independence disclaimer minimize some risks, **but they do not establish permission or contract compliance**. For high-confidence legal clearance, seek written publisher authorization or qualified legal advice, especially regarding the Terms' AI/data and game-use restrictions. Do not upload game assets, screenshots, source code or internal data.
- **Case-study claim:** "I analyzed public descriptions of a star-gated reward-crate mechanic and proposed a hypothetical economy test" — not "I optimized Playtika's game economy."

## Source-use and AI caution

This case uses manually curated high-level facts from publicly accessible official descriptions and independently published third-party articles; it does **not** copy/ingest game executables or scrape in-game user data. Nonetheless, the Terms' AI/data restrictions merit separate attention before publication, especially if an AI-assisted tool processes content from the game or publisher site. Our recommendation to publish a text-only independent case is a **risk-reduction approach**, not confirmation that Playtika permits it. Avoid implying game-approved or license-cleared status.
